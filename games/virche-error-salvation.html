Synopsis ♡

In the isolated island nation of Arpéchéle, people are cursed to die at the age of 23. Known as the "God of Death," Ceres carries a mysterious curse that brings misfortune to those around her.

Surrounded by death and despair, Ceres encounters individuals connected to her fate while uncovering the hidden truths behind the island, her curse, and the mysterious organization seeking to change their inevitable destinies.
