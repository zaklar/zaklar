Synopsis ♡

In the isolated island nation of Arpéchéle, people are cursed to die at the age of 23. Known as the "God of Death," Ceres carries a mysterious curse that brings misfortune to those around her.

As she encounters individuals connected to her fate, Ceres begins to uncover the dark secrets of the island, the truth behind her curse, and the mysteries surrounding her own existence.

Virche Evermore -Error: Salvation- is the original story entry that introduces the tragic world of Arpéchéle, its doomed inhabitants, and the paths of those who seek salvation from their unavoidable fate.
